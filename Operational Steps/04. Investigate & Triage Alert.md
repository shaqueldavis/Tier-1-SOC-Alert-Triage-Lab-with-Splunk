#  Investigate & Triage Alert

### Process

As a SOC Analyst we want to follow a repeatable process while investigating this alert:
```text
✅ Alert triggered
     ↓
➡️ Initial documentation
     ↓
Validate the alert
     ↓
Establish scope
     ↓
Identify account + source
     ↓
Determine logon type
     ↓
Look for related activity
     ↓
Assess malicious vs. benign
     ↓
Determine severity/disposition
     ↓
Document findings
     ↓
Escalate or close
```

Our goal is to answer What happened, who/what was involved, how significant is it, and what should happen next?

This is where we shift our mind. Naturally, we know everything that just happened because we engineered the detection. However, we are switching hats and approaching this from the perspective that we are now a SOC analyst at work. One who has not seen this alert before. From that point of view, we now hold the responsibility of Investigating the legitimacy and severity of an unknown potential threat.

### Validate The Alert

Now we move on the next step in our process:
```text
✅ Alert triggered
     ↓
✅ Initial documentation
     ↓
➡️ Validate the alert
     ↓
Establish scope
     ↓
Identify account + source
     ↓
Determine logon type
     ↓
Look for related activity
     ↓
Assess malicious vs. benign
     ↓
Determine severity/disposition
     ↓
Document findings
     ↓
Escalate or close
```

The first thing we want to do is reproduce the alert. This confirms that the alert wasn't a false trigger caused by the alert configuration.
```spl
index=* host="<WINDOWS-HOSTNAME>" EventCode=4625
| bin _time span=5m
| stats count as failed_logins by _time host
| where failed_logins >= 5
```
In my case:
```spl
index=* host="Splunk_Lab" EventCode=4625
| bin _time span=5m
| stats count as failed_logins by _time host
| where failed_logins >= 5
```
